# Seraphine · 栖薇的琉璃玫瑰圣堂档案夹

An interactive stained-glass rose & gothic frosted-glass sanctuary folder experience (`Seraphine · 栖薇 · 9 阕圣咏残章`), featuring:
- **Dual Cathedral Themes**: `晨曦欧泊` (Opaline Candlelight & Cerulean Vitrail) & `午夜圣血` (Crimson Nocturne & Baroque Gold Filigree Frame).
- **3/4 Cupped Vitrail Rose Engine**: Depth-sorted stained-glass petals, champagne-gold filigree rims, thorny spring stems, cascading right-edge vine & drooping rosebud.
- **Breathing Cathedral Tyndall Light Shafts**, living bokeh orbs & droplets, 3D-fluttering crystal rose petals, and procedural **Cathedral Celesta** music-box audio.
